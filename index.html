Knowing what a service does—and what it doesn’t—is part of feeling confident using it.

Is Welflows a government service?

No. Welflows is a fictional, independent startup concept. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Korean government. Official external links are provided as separate next-step resources.

Can I apply for benefits here?

No. This demo helps you explore support topics and prepare questions. It does not accept applications, verify eligibility, calculate benefits, or access government records. Confirm details with the relevant official service before acting.

Do I need to share personal information?

No account, name, identification number, contact details, or financial information is requested. This demo keeps your topic selections only in temporary page memory. They are not sent to a server.

Can I use this to support someone else?

Yes. Use the finder for yourself, a family member, or someone you support. The professional toolkit also offers a generic conversation checklist. Please do not enter or upload anyone’s personal information.
